**Sunshore** — an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. You are Rook, a driver in debt to Mara Voss, who runs the Sunset Diner and half the town's business. Explore the sun-soaked coastal city of Sunshore on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police, and work through five story missions — ending with a robbery of Marina Bank.

What's in the game:

- A full open-world city: roads, traffic, pedestrians, a beach and marina, parks and palm trees
- Drive and steal cars (walk up to one and press **E**), on foot or behind the wheel
- A wanted level (1–5 stars) with police cars and foot cops who chase, shoot and arrest
- Five story missions with dialogue, objectives and map markers: First Wheels, Courier Run, Debt Collection, Hot Pursuit, The Big Score
- Combat: pistol (mouse aim, click to shoot) and fists (**F**), enemy crews, health and cash drops
- HUD with a live minimap, wanted stars, health, money, clock and mission objective
- Title screen, pause, Busted/Wasted screens, and localStorage saves (Continue where you left off)
- Synthesized WebAudio sound: sirens, engine hum, gunshots, mission jingles — no audio files
- 12 hidden sun coins worth $25 each, and free roam after the story ends

Everything is original: the city, characters, vehicles, story, art and sounds are invented for this game. No names, characters, maps or assets from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or just open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests.

Controls: **WASD / arrows** move and drive · **Mouse** aim · **Click** shoot · **F** punch · **E** steal / enter / exit a car · **Shift** run · **P / Esc** pause · **M** mute.
